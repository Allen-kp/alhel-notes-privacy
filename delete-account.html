AlHEl Notes Account and Data Deletion

Last Updated: June 2026

AlHEl Notes is an offline-first note-taking application. Users may optionally sign in with Google to enable backup and restore through their own Google Drive account.

How to Delete Your Data

Delete Cloud Backup
Open AlHEl Notes and navigate to:

Settings → Delete Cloud Backup

This permanently deletes AlHEl Notes backup files stored in your Google Drive account.
Remove Google Account Access
You may disconnect your Google account from within the app or revoke access through your Google Account settings.
Delete Local Notes
Notes stored on your device can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling AlHEl Notes.

Data Retention

AlHEl Notes does not maintain developer-controlled user accounts or store user notes on developer-owned servers. Backup data is stored in the user's own Google Drive account until deleted by the user.
